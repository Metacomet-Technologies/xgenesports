Our Story

Built in the Arena. Built for the People.

Two Rocket League players met in 2020 and turned a random ranked queue into something bigger — a community, a culture, and an enterprise built by rebels who refuse to play it safe. Shibby and Ender are the owners.

The Origin

It started with a queue

In 2020, Shibby (DudeShibby) and Ender (b00nd0ck3r) crossed paths in a Rocket League lobby — the kind of match that shouldn't matter, but somehow does. No org backing. No sponsors. Just two players who clicked: same pace, same hunger, same refusal to accept "good enough."

What began as late-night ranked sessions turned into something neither of them planned. They weren't just stacking wins — they were building a crew. Teammates who showed up. Friends who talked trash and backed it up. A social environment where competitive drive and real community could exist in the same room.

From that foundation, XGeN eSports grew into a full enterprise — rosters across multiple titles, tournaments, coaching, clip culture, and a Discord that never sleeps. But the DNA hasn't changed. It's still the same energy from that first lobby: rebels, shakers, and disruptors who'd rather build something new than fit into something old.

The Journey

From lobby to enterprise

2020
Shibby & Ender meet in Rocket League

A random ranked match connects DudeShibby and b00nd0ck3r — two Steam players with the same competitive fire. Late-night queues turn into a Discord server, and the first XGeN squad takes shape.

2021–2023
Community becomes culture

Clip drops, scrims, and in-house tournaments turn a friend group into a real org. New games, new rosters — but the same rebel energy that started it all.

2024–2025
Scaling the enterprise

Five game divisions, coaching programs, merch drops, and competitive rosters across Rocket League, Valorant, CS2, Apex, and Modern Warfare. Structure meets soul.

2026
The arena has arrived

XGeN eSports goes live at xgenesports.com — a home base for the community, the clips, the matches, and everyone who believes competitive gaming should feel like family.
