Last updated: August 19, 2026

XGeN eSports ("we," "us," or "our") operates xgenesports.com and our community Discord server. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or participate in our community.

We may collect limited information when you interact with our site or Discord, including:

We use collected information to operate and improve our website, run community events, moderate Discord, display leaderboards and clips, communicate with members, and maintain a safe competitive environment.

Cookies & Analytics

Our site may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality, performance, and security. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only when required to operate the community, comply with law, enforce our Terms, or protect the safety of our members.

Children's Privacy

Our community is intended for a general gaming audience. If you are under 13, please do not provide personal information without parental consent. Contact us on Discord if you believe a minor has submitted personal data.

Your Choices

You may leave our Discord server at any time, request removal of community-submitted content where applicable, and adjust your browser or Discord privacy settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
